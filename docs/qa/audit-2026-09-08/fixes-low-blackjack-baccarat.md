## Boundaries and limits

The backend mission route already verifies the configured contract and matching player, then records proof through its existing persistence flow; the casino mission is a daily boolean. No backend eligibility change was needed. The actual verifier was executed locally, but authenticated production HTTP/Redis persistence and a live natural wager were not exercised.

The Blackjack snapshot/read and Baccarat settlement controllers remain separate, game-specific implementations. This change consolidates confirmed duplicated action rendering, lifecycle status, preference handling and presentation ownership; it does not replace the financial state logic wholesale. P1 prepared-action stability, no-new-spend recovery, raw payouts, owner/land/token result identity and the explicit uncertainty of resumed split Double history remain intact. Global typecheck/build/full-suite integration is owned by the parent task.
