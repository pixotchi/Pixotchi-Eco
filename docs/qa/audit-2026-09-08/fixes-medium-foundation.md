It covers direct/invalid quantity purchases, short-viewport final menu choice, large asset search and selection preservation, exact amount/clipboard failure, late portaled scrolling and text-only growth, tablet width, and the actual MintShareModal field with its API intercepted before any mutation.

Final integrated matrix and actual-app results are recorded in the parent ledger.
